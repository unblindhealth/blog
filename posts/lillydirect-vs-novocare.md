**LillyDirect and NovoCare are manufacturer-backed ways to get certain GLP-1 prescriptions, but neither is automatically the cheapest option.**

LillyDirect is Eli Lilly's platform. NovoCare is Novo Nordisk's. Both can offer lower self-pay prices than list price, but the drugs, dose pricing and insurance rules differ.

The simplest approach: **start with the drug you were prescribed, then compare your insurance price with the manufacturer's price.**

## Which GLP-1s can you get through each program?

| Program | Examples with current direct/self-pay pricing | Current price range verified Sept. 11, 2026 |
|---|---|---:|
| **LillyDirect** | Foundayo, Zepbound, Mounjaro | Foundayo about **$149–$299/month**; Zepbound about **$299–$449/month** under current program terms; Mounjaro starts at **$499/month** |
| **NovoCare** | Wegovy, Ozempic | Wegovy pill **$149–$299/month**; Wegovy injection generally **$199 intro / $349–$399 standard depending on dose**; Ozempic generally **$199 intro / $349–$499 depending on dose** |

Those numbers are not interchangeable. Foundayo, Wegovy and Zepbound have weight-management indications. Ozempic and Mounjaro are diabetes medicines with different FDA-approved uses.

Your prescriber decides which medicine is appropriate. The shopping decision starts **after** you know what was prescribed.

## Does manufacturer-direct mean you skip insurance?

No.

LillyDirect can support insurance and self-pay pathways depending on the medicine. NovoCare also offers insurance savings programs, while its NovoCare Pharmacy cash/self-pay transactions are processed outside insurance.

That creates two very different routes:

**Insurance route:** Your plan processes the prescription. A manufacturer savings card may reduce your remaining patient responsibility if you qualify.

**Self-pay route:** You pay a manufacturer-backed cash price outside insurance.

If you use the self-pay route, do not assume the payment will count toward your insurance deductible or out-of-pocket maximum.

## What changed for Medicare in 2026?

This is where the comparison changes substantially.

Starting July 1, 2026, the **Medicare GLP-1 Bridge** gives eligible people with Part D access to:

- **Foundayo tablets**
- **Wegovy injection or tablets**
- **Zepbound KwikPen**

for a **$50 monthly copay**.

That means an eligible Medicare patient should generally check the Bridge or regular Part D coverage **before** choosing LillyDirect or NovoCare self-pay.

The Bridge is not normal Part D coverage. Its $50 copay does not count toward the Part D deductible or true out-of-pocket costs.

Medicare also says patients prescribed a GLP-1 for a Part D-coverable indication — such as Zepbound for moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea or Wegovy for certain cardiovascular-risk reduction — should use the regular Part D pathway rather than the Bridge.

## Which program is cheaper?

There is no useful "LillyDirect vs NovoCare winner" because the programs usually sell **different prescriptions**.

Compare this instead:

**Your prescription → insurance price → manufacturer savings offer → manufacturer self-pay price → pharmacy cash price**

For example, NovoCare's lowest Wegovy pill price does not make Wegovy the "cheapest" choice for someone prescribed Zepbound. Changing medication is a clinical decision.

## What are the catches to check?

Manufacturer prices often come with conditions:

- A price may apply only to certain doses.
- A low introductory price may last only a few fills.
- A higher-dose discount may require refilling within a set number of days.
- Commercial savings cards may exclude government-insured patients.
- Self-pay purchases may not count toward insurance cost-sharing totals.

The headline number is only useful after you know **which version of the offer applies to you**.

## What should I do before paying?

Ask for three numbers:

1. **Your actual insurance patient responsibility**
2. **The manufacturer price for your exact drug and dose**
3. **The pharmacy cash price**

Then ask whether choosing the manufacturer self-pay price takes the prescription outside your insurance benefits.

For a full price comparison, see [GLP-1 cost without insurance](/blog/glp-1-cost-without-insurance).

## Bottom line

LillyDirect and NovoCare make GLP-1 pricing more visible. That is useful.

But manufacturer-direct is **another price to compare**, not a reason to automatically bypass insurance.

For Medicare patients especially, check the new 2026 federal coverage pathways first.
